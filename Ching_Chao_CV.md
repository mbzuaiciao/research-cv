## Selected AI Research & Engineering Projects

**[Institutional Risk Agent Fleet](https://github.com/mbzuaiciao/institutional-risk-agent-fleet)**  
Built a hackathon multi-agent risk-investigation prototype with Gemini / Google ADK: specialist investigators, an adversarial challenger, deterministic claim verification, and human approval. Implemented Cloud Run, Pub/Sub, Firestore, role-bound permissions, audit trails, and a demo; 66 tests reported. Synthetic data; not a deployed trading or investment system. [Devpost](https://devpost.com/software/institutional-risk-agent-fleet).

**[Institutional Investment Agents](https://github.com/mbzuaiciao/institutional-investment-agents)**  
Studied whether specialist agents, critique, and verification improve research quality rather than merely response complexity; evaluated architectures in controlled synthetic tasks. Completed a 324-run model-capability × harness-capability simulator factorial; developed a real-model validation protocol (empirical real-model results pending).

**[AFML Research Harness](https://github.com/mbzuaiciao/afml-research-harness)**  
Built a zero-trust research evaluator with leakage controls, multiple-testing corrections, reproducible reporting, and provenance; EXP-011 probes adaptive-search overfitting. Completed deterministic R0/R1 baseline; self-hosted external-model confirmatory testing remains in progress.
